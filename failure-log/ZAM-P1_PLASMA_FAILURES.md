# ZAM-P1 — Plasma Failures (Preserved)

This file records the preserved failure entries from the P1 Plasma Power System Research Campaign. Do not delete.

PF-01: Pure plasma energy source without primary energy input
Classification: FUNDAMENTALLY FAILED
Summary: Any claim that plasma alone (without an external primary energy input) constitutes a net energy source violates conservation of energy. Plasma is a state of matter/process, not an energy reservoir.

PF-02: Open-cycle MHD as lightweight 250–350 kW vehicle primary converter
Classification: ENGINEERING / TECHNOLOGICAL FAILURE
Summary: While open-cycle MHD can convert kinetic/thermal energy to electricity, vehicle-scale continuous operation requires magnets, seed systems, electrodes, and cooling whose mass and thermal penalties render the approach unattractive for the target envelope.

PF-03: Closed-cycle plasma/MHD without external heat source
Classification: ENERGY-SOURCE FAILURE
Summary: Closed-cycle concepts require an external high-temperature heat source; omission of that source in energy accounting is invalid.

PF-04: Pulsed plasma as continuous shaft-power source
Classification: SYSTEM-LEVEL FAILURE
Summary: Pulsed plasma devices redistribute stored electrical energy (capacitors/batteries); they are not independent continuous power generators at vehicle scale.

PF-05: Plasma reforming used as a net energy generator
Classification: ENERGY-BALANCE FAILURE
Summary: On-board plasma reforming typically consumes more energy than the recoverable chemical or electrical benefit once full system losses are included.

PF-06: Plasma-heated Brayton cycle as a lightweight vehicle prime mover
Classification: MASS / EFFICIENCY FAILURE
Summary: Wall-plug inefficiencies and thermal rejection challenges currently negate the theoretical benefits when sized for vehicle integration.

PF-07: Direct plasma-to-electricity converter without a suitable primary high-energy plasma source
Classification: SOURCE / CONVERSION LIMITATION
Summary: Direct conversion requires a high-energy plasma which itself must be produced by a primary energy source; conversion inefficiencies and space-charge issues limit vehicle applicability.
