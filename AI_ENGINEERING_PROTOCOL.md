Principles
----------
NO SINGLE AI IS AUTHORITATIVE SIMPLY BECAUSE IT PRODUCES A CONFIDENT ANSWER.

Major engineering decisions should be handled as follows:
1. Proposed.
2. Independently reviewed where appropriate.
3. Challenged.
4. Calculated or researched.
5. Resolved.
6. Recorded.
7. Approved.
8. Versioned.

Notes
-----
- All engineering statements must be traceable to a session ID and supporting evidence (calculation, simulation, test, or literature).
- Labels for provenance: UNKNOWN / ASSUMPTION / PROVISIONAL / CALCULATED / VALIDATED
