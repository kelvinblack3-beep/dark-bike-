# 05 - Governing Equations

Purpose
- Collect the fundamental equations used across analyses. Keep derivations separate in the calculations/ folder.

Examples (non-exhaustive):
- Conservation of mass: continuity equation
- Conservation of momentum: Navier–Stokes (and MHD-modified forms)
- Conservation of energy: energy equation including Joule heating, radiative terms
- Maxwell's equations (differential form)
- Ohm's law (generalized for plasma and MHD contexts)
- Saha equation (for ionization equilibrium estimates)
- Boltzmann / kinetic relations as needed for non-equilibrium plasmas

Notes
- Include assumptions for each equation (e.g., steady-state, quasi-neutrality, single-fluid MHD vs kinetic).
