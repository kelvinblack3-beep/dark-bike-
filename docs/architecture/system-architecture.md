Primary Vehicle Architecture
----------------------------

ENGINE
  ↓
CLUTCH
  ↓
GEARBOX
  ↓
FINAL DRIVE
  ↓
REAR WHEEL

Notes
-----
- The above represents the required functional chain for basic vehicle propulsion and must be able to operate without any experimental systems.


Experimental / Modular Systems
-----------------------------
- X1 — FRONT AIR MANAGEMENT (modular experimental front fan and air-management system)
- X2 — REAR EXHAUST AUGMENTATION
- X3 — ACTIVE AERODYNAMICS
- X4 — ADVANCED EXHAUST RESEARCH

These experimental systems are modular augmentation systems and must not be required for basic vehicle operation, steering, braking, or fundamental stability.
