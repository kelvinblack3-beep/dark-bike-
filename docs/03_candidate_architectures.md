# 03 - Candidate Architectures

Purpose
- Record candidate powertrain and system architectures, with current status and summary reasons.

Current candidate list (from Division I review):
- Plasma-assisted combustion — ACCEPTED PROVISIONALLY
  - Rationale: physically defensible, low added plasma hardware mass, potential efficiency improvements.
- Liquid-metal MHD + plasma heat source — POSSIBLE BUT UNATTRACTIVE
  - Rationale: mass of magnets, pumps and heat exchangers currently excessive.
- Plasma-assisted engine + liquid-metal MHD waste-heat recovery — POTENTIALLY VIABLE / REQUIRES RESEARCH
- Microwave air plasma thruster — REJECTED (onboard energy balance does not close)
- Plasma-heated Brayton cycle — REJECTED / LOW PRIORITY
- Direct plasma-to-electricity/Post converter — REJECTED FOR CURRENT VEHICLE SCALE
- On-board plasma hydrogen reforming — REJECTED PROVISIONALLY
- Tandem-mirror plasma rocket adapted to terrestrial power — REJECTED
- TELEC laser/plasma converter — REJECTED

Guidance
- Do not re-open a rejected concept without new evidence. Preserve failure rationale in docs/18_rejected_concepts.md.
