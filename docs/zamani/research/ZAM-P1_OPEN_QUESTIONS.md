# ZAM-P1 — Open Questions

This file lists the remaining open research questions from the ZAM-P1 Plasma Power System Research Campaign.

1. What is the independently verified real-world BSFC / efficiency benefit of nanosecond plasma ignition under representative engine operating cycles?
2. What is the actual electrical energy consumed per representative combustion cycle for PAC in production-like settings?
3. What is the lifetime and reliability of plasma ignition hardware at high engine speeds and temperatures?
4. What is the measured mass of a complete automotive-grade plasma ignition subsystem (power electronics, cabling, cooling, mounting)?
5. Can PAC enable sufficiently lean/high-EGR operation to materially improve total vehicle powertrain efficiency when electrical and control penalties are included?
6. Can liquid-metal MHD (LMMHD) waste-heat recovery produce net electrical power after accounting for pumps, magnets, heat exchangers, power electronics, pressure and thermal losses?
7. Can an LMMHD system mass be reduced enough to justify recovered power at vehicle scale?
8. Are published MHD power-density values being confused with component-level or laboratory-scale numbers that omit system-level mass and auxiliaries?
