# 06 - Energy Accounting

Purpose
- Template and examples for bounding the full energy flows in proposed architectures.

Sections
- Input energy sources (chemical, stored electrical, external)
- Conversion steps and efficiencies
- Useful output (shaft power, electrical power, thrust)
- Loss channels (radiation, conduction, conduction to coolant, unconverted chemical energy)
- Integrated energy balance examples

Notes
- Every architecture must close an energy balance to within stated uncertainty before moving past the first gate.
