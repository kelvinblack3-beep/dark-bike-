# 08 - Electromagnetic Analysis

Purpose
- Templates and baseline models for electromagnetic field analysis, coupling, and forces.

Sections
- Field generation mechanisms and sources
- Coupling to plasma (Lorentz force, ponderomotive effects)
- Inductance, capacitance, skin-depth estimates
- EMI and shielding considerations

Notes
- Keep analyses symbolic and order-of-magnitude until validated by simulation or experiment.
