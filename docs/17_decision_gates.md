# 17 - Decision Gates

Purpose
- Define program decision gates and the required evidence for progression.

Gate 0: Research kickoff — documentation and organization (complete)
Gate 1: "Does a physically credible plasma architecture exist that could plausibly scale to useful power?"
  - Required evidence:
    - First-principles energy closure (order-of-magnitude)
    - Momentum and thermal budgeting
    - Material feasibility at required temperatures/fields
    - Simulation plan for identified uncertainties
    - Adversarial review by Division III
  - If "pass": permit limited low-risk simulations and surrogate experiments (subject to safety review)
  - If "fail": archive the concept with detailed failure reasoning

Further gates (TBD): modelling validation, sub-system demonstration, vehicle integration, safety approval.
