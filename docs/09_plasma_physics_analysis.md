# 09 - Plasma Physics Analysis

Purpose
- Template for plasma-specific analysis (ionization, collisionality, transport phenomena).

Sections
- Regime classification (collisional/ collisionless, magnetized/unmagnetized)
- Ionization balance, electron/ion temperatures
- Mean free path, Debye length, plasma frequency
- Transport coefficients (conductivity, viscosity, thermal conductivity)
- Non-equilibrium considerations

Notes
- Avoid procedural detail; focus on physics assumptions, scaling, and closure.
