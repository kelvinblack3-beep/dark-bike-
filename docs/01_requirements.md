# 01 - Requirements (Template)

Purpose
- Record system-level and vehicle-level requirements that any architecture must meet before acceptance.

Top-level targets (provisional)
- Vehicle target peak power: 250–350 kW (provisional)
- Vehicle target curb/ready-to-run mass: 500–700 kg (provisional)
- Single-seat, track-focused vehicle

Requirement categories
- Functional: power, torque, range, acceleration, continuous power.
- Non-functional: safety, manufacturability, maintainability, reliability, environmental.
- Constraints: materials, thermal limits, packaging limits, regulatory.

Traceability
- Each requirement must include a rationale and source, and be traceable to ADRs and trade studies.

Notes
- These figures are provisional and must be validated by independent calculations and closure checks in Division III.
