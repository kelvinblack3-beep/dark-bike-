# 18 - Rejected Concepts and Reasons

Purpose
- Maintain the documented failures and reasons so they remain available for future reference.

Current archive (summarized from Division I):
- Microwave air plasma thruster — Rejected: onboard energy source cannot close energy balance.
- Plasma-heated Brayton cycle — Rejected / Low priority: wall-plug losses and thermal rejection undermine advantage.
- Direct thermoelectric/Nernst plasma conversion — Rejected: low conversion efficiency and excessive mass.
- On-board plasma hydrogen reforming — Rejected Provisionally: energy balance and conversion losses unattractive.
- Direct plasma-to-electricity/Post converter — Rejected for current vehicle scale: space-charge and ion-energy limitations.
- Tandem-mirror plasma rocket adapted to terrestrial power — Rejected: magnetic confinement/mass problem.
- TELEC laser/plasma converter — Rejected: requires external laser energy source.
