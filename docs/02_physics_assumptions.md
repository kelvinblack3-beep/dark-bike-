# 02 - Physics Assumptions

Purpose
- List physics-level assumptions that will be used across analyses. Make them explicit and numerically stated where possible.

Common assumptions (to be reviewed and refined):
- Ambient reference temperature: 293 K (unless otherwise stated)
- Atmospheric pressure: 101325 Pa
- Vehicle operational envelope: track conditions; specify ranges for ambient temperature and altitude in studies
- Plasma assumptions: list of regimes (collisionality, degree of ionization, quasi-neutrality assumptions)
- Magnetic field assumptions: typical field strengths, scaling relationships
- Material property baselines: reference against open literature

Change control
- Any change to physics assumptions must be logged with date, author, and justification.
